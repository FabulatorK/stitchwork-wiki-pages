Companion Mods

referencecreator5 min read
For Stitchwork 0.9.1 · Last edited: 2026-10-22

Reference · for creators.
Mods and programs that work well beside Stitchwork, and the job each one does best.

Stitchwork changes what the game shows, not how it runs. So mods that help you control the game's state while you work run well beside it. This page lists the mods and programs that we tested with Stitchwork or built Stitchwork around. The list will grow.

DebugMod

The community's all-purpose debugging mod, and the most useful companion for creators. Two of its features matter most:

  • Savestates. Snapshot the game at a moment, and return to it as many times as you want. Essential for testing replacements that only trigger once — first-meeting dialogue, one-shot cutscene audio, a boss's intro. The full walkthrough is Testing With Savestates.
  • Global time-scale pause. Freeze or slow the whole game while Stitchwork's tools stay live. A busy scene with effects too fast to catch? Freeze time mid-attack. Then hide DebugMod's panels, because Stitchwork's keys open nothing while they show (see below). Then use Pick object in world (Graphics → Animation) or Pick texture in world (Graphics → T2D Textures) to click the exact sprite on screen. The P key starts World Pick for both.

Stitchwork and DebugMod are tested together, and Stitchwork handles the mouse cursor as DebugMod does. But the two mods do not show their panels at the same time:

  • When DebugMod's panels appear, Stitchwork's panels close, the Pack Manager (Packs) and Settings included. The Pack Manager keeps your staged changes. Pick mode stops.
  • Stitchwork's pull tabs and status badge hide while DebugMod's panels show.
  • Stitchwork's keys open nothing while DebugMod's panels show. A notice tells you what to do: hide DebugMod's panels with DebugMod's toggle key, then press the Stitchwork key again.

Benchwarp, FsmMaster, Architect and UnityExplorer

These mods show menus or editors of their own. While one of these menus or editors shows, Stitchwork steps aside, as it does for DebugMod (above). When you press a Stitchwork key, a notice tells you what to do.

  • Benchwarp: its menu, which shows while the game is paused. Resume the game to get Stitchwork back.
  • FsmMaster: its overlay (F3). A digit that you type in its search field does not act as a Stitchwork key.
  • Architect: its edit mode. The notice shows at the top right, clear of Architect's editor, and names Architect's edit key. Architect uses the keys 1 to 9 for its tools. For these keys, the notice shows only the first time. The letter E typed in a Stitchwork text field does not toggle Architect's editor. When Architect finishes loading its objects, your skins apply, and the progress plate shows.
  • UnityExplorer and CinematicUnityExplorer: their menu (F7). By default, Explorer shows its menu when the game starts. Press F7 to hide it, and Stitchwork's pull tabs come back. To keep Explorer's menu and Stitchwork's panels open together, switch off Step aside for in Settings › General (that setting shows only when one of them is installed). Then you cannot type in Stitchwork's text fields while Explorer's menu shows, because Explorer takes the keyboard each frame.

Custom Knight Creator (CKC)

A standalone skin-creation program that runs outside the game. It was made for Hollow Knight, and it now works with Silksong assets. It shows the sprites of each animation, previews the animation, and lets you check that the frames flow smoothly before you export.

Stitchwork is not a replacement for CKC, and doesn't want to be. If CKC is your comfortable home, keep making skins there — the Spritesheets/ tier loads CKC output as-is, and Stitchwork's individual files can layer fixes on top of a CKC port without re-exporting. The full porting story: Porting Prior Work.

HornetCloakColor

Michael4d45's cloak recolor — pick any color for Hornet's cloak. It marks the cloak's pixels with a mask and tints them as the game draws them. Here is how it works beside Stitchwork:

  • Works out of the box alongside Stitchwork, including with skin packs that recolor or repaint Hornet at vanilla sprite sizes — the tint applies over the pack's art.
  • Skins with oversized Hornet sprites (art made bigger with anchors or the Animation Canvas) switch its recolor off for now. These skins make the atlas bigger, and HornetCloakColor does not tint an atlas of a size that it does not know. Its log then says "size does not match atlas". For now, use the recolor or the oversized skin on a save, not both. A fix suggestion is with the author.
  • Heavily redrawn cloaks recolor along the vanilla cloak's outline (the mask marks where the vanilla cloak is). Skins that only change the colors work best with it.

Stitchwork also has Hornet color settings of its own: Graphics → Colors → Hornet, with the Saturation, Hue and Brightness levers and the Silk tiers switch. They save to Recolor/_hero.txt. See Colors.

KnightInSilksong

If KnightInSilksong's HUD shows magenta blocks, update Stitchwork.

Hollow Knight skins for the Custom Knight mod can become KnightInSilksong packs. In the Pack Manager, click Convert on a Custom Knight skin. This makes a new pack, <skin> (Knight) (Installing Packs). Ask the skin's creator before you share a converted pack.

Not companions

Some mods replace the same assets as Stitchwork: Patchwork, Customizer and its variants, the font mods SilksongFontsInject and CustomFontSilksong, and audio mods such as CustomAudio. We recommend that you don't run them beside Stitchwork. The full list is in Getting Started.

Do you know a mod or tool that works beside Stitchwork and saves creators time? Tell us in the #Stitchwork channel of the Hollow Knight Modding Discord server.
