A0 About Stitchwork

introplayercreator2 min read
For Stitchwork 0.9.0 · Last edited: 2026-10-16

Foundations · everyone.
Where Stitchwork comes from, and the people it stands on.

The short version

Stitchwork is a humble fork of Patchwork by Ashiepaws. This project is very grateful to Ashiepaws. Most features in this project are built from user feedback on Patchwork.

Patchwork, and Ashiepaws

Patchwork pioneered live asset replacement for Hollow Knight: Silksong — sprites, audio, text and video, swapped on the fly, and it never edits the game's asset files. Stitchwork inherited its loading model, its pack idea, and its replace-don't-modify approach. Ideas from Ashiepaws continue to shape the roadmap.

Two behaviors follow from that lineage:

  • Patchwork packs load unchanged. Stitchwork discovers Patchwork/ folders exactly as it discovers its own. A pack made for Patchwork works with no changes.
  • If Patchwork itself is installed, Stitchwork goes inert for the session rather than fight over the same game hooks.

What the fork adds

On that foundation, Stitchwork adds its own creator toolset:

  • A new interface — pull tabs drop down from the top edge of the screen, and each tool opens in docked panels.
  • World Pick — press P, then click an object on screen to open it in the right resource browser / editor.
  • Animation Canvas — a whole clip on one expanded, aligned, uniform canvas; paint it, then compile it back to per-frame sprites.
  • Appliqué — per-clip art, even when a clip shares frames with other clips.
  • Pack Doctor — a static check of a pack's files, with a report.
  • Boot health check — game-version match and more, on the Dashboard.
  • UI localization — Simplified Chinese available. Pick the language in Settings › General › Language (key 0, or the gear tab): Follow the game, English, Chinese, or any other language file. More on the way from community contributors.
  • Translate — Text → Translate lets anyone translate Stitchwork's interface. New language… starts a new one. Your file goes in Lang/ in your working folder, so a mod update leaves it alone.

Next: Getting Started.

Thanks

Beyond its predecessor, Stitchwork is shaped by many hands:

  • The skin creators and testers whose packs, bug reports, and screenshots drove nearly every fix and feature in this wiki — the change history is, honestly, a conversation with them.
  • The localization helpers: the Chinese terminology work that the in-game glossary and this wiki's zh pages are built on, and the German translation now under way.
  • Fellow toolmakers whose published work taught us techniques: dakusan (SilkDev — the world-pick projection approach), the DebugMod authors (cursor handling, and the pause workflow every creator uses), YLTai and CarrieForle (whose font mods shaped Stitchwork's font-handling boundaries), and Custom Knight Creator from the Hollow Knight era — the animation canvas's spiritual ancestor.
  • The lineage before the lineage, inherited with Patchwork's own thanks: Douglas Gregory (the GPU performance work the tk2d sprite path is built on), RatherChaoticDev (Silksong Customizer, which Patchwork grew from), and Su4enka (CustomizerT2D, which shaped the T2D support Stitchwork extends).
