Dumping & Full Dump

referencecreator5 min read
For Stitchwork 0.9.1 · Last edited: 2026-10-22

Reference · for creators.
Save the game's own art and text to disk as your reference.
Do one scene at a time, or the whole game at once.

Dumps are a reference, not pack content. Stitchwork never loads from Dumps/. You copy files from it when you start an edit. The Pack Doctor (Check this pack, the stethoscope on each pack card) also compares the names in your packs with your dumps, to find typos.

The integrity guarantee

Dumps always export the game's original art and text, even while packs or your own working-folder edits are active. You never have to turn off your packs to get a clean dump.

The controls (Utilities → Dumping panel)

Open Utilities (7, or the Utilities pull tab). The Dumping panel sits at the right. Its Open folder button opens Dumps/ in your file browser. When the panel is too short for all of its parts, scroll it.

ControlWhat it does
Sprites on scene load (switch, "slower loads")Every scene you enter dumps its sprites to Dumps/Sprites/. Slows scene loads noticeably — leave OFF during normal play.
All game text → Dump all text (button)Exports every text sheet in every language to Dumps/Text/. While the dump runs, the game's language changes, then changes back. At the end, a message shows Text dumped with the counts of languages, sheets and entries, and the time. If a file write fails, the message shows Text dump stopped and the reason.
Full dump (button, "~20 min")Walks every scene in the game and dumps each one. The button is not available until Sprites on scene load is on and a game is in progress. The first click arms it, and it reads Click to start. Click again to start. Details: below.
Convert sheets (button)Splits loaded whole-sheet replacements into individual sprite PNGs under Converted/ When Only sprites that differ from vanilla is on, it writes only the frames that the sheet changes. Use it to change a ported sheet into individual sprites (B3).

If Converted/ already has files, a dialog asks first (B3).

Per-sprite and per-atlas dumping also lives where you browse: in Graphics → Animation, the Edit (new from vanilla) and Ready N sprites for edit buttons; in Graphics → T2D Textures, the Dump row with Atlas, All sprites and Sprite (B2).

Full dump, what to expect

  • Start it from a game in progress. Load a save, then start the dump from the world. At the main menu the button is not available, and the line under it says "Load a save first: the full dump walks the world from a game in progress." When the dump cannot start, a press of FullDumpKey shows the same reason at once, in a Full dump not started message.
  • A progress plate appears at the bottom center: Full dump, the SCENE counter, the current scene with its collection count, and the elapsed minutes. The screen shows each scene in turn while the dump moves through the game. A full run takes many minutes and writes several gigabytes of PNGs.
  • The pull tabs hide while the dump runs, and the game's own controls are off. Every Stitchwork panel closes, and Stitchwork's keys open nothing. A press of one shows "The full dump is running. Hold Bksp to cancel it, or wait for it to end."
  • When the run ends, its result stays on the plate for 20 seconds.
  • When the dump ends, Stitchwork returns you to the main menu and does not save. Full dump never writes your save file, so play since your last save is lost. Click Continue to play again. The dump visits every room, and a room can change your save's progress when you enter it. From the start of the dump to the main menu, Stitchwork blocks every save, autosave, achievement and menu style unlock. A copy of a 100%-complete save is still the nicest one to run it on — the more the world has opened up, the more of it there is to dump — but that's about coverage, not safety.
  • Changed your mind? Hold Backspace for about a second to cancel (FullDumpCancelKey in the config). The plate says "Hold Bksp to cancel · progress is saved", and a red fill moves across the plate while you hold the key. Here, "progress" is the dump's list of finished scenes, not your save file. What was dumped so far stays on disk.
  • A new run continues where the last one stopped. It skips the scenes that earlier runs completed (the list is Dumps/.fulldump-progress.txt). When every scene is done, the next run starts fresh.
  • The full-dump key (FullDumpKey, unbound by default) starts a full dump too. Hold it for 1.5 seconds: the hold is its confirm step, so a tap does nothing. While you hold it, the plate shows Full dump, the text "Your session ends here; the dump stops at the main menu", and a fill that grows while you hold. To bind this key or to change the cancel key, open Settings (key 0, or the gear tab at the right end of the pull tabs). The two keys are under Keys: Full dump and Cancel a dump.

Where files land

<Game folder>/Stitchwork/
  Dumps/
    Sprites/{Collection}/{Material}/{Sprite}.png   ← tk2d frames
    Sprites/T2D/{AtlasName}/{Sprite}.png           ← T2D atlas sprites
    Sprites/T2D/_standalone/{Texture}.png          ← non-atlas and sprite-less textures
    Spritesheets/T2D/{Name}.png                    ← full T2D atlases
    Text/{Sheet}/{LANG}.yml                        ← every string, every language
    Sounds/{Clip}.wav                              ← Dump WAV (Audio tab)

Inside Dumps/, the folders have the same layout as your working folder. Copy a file from Dumps/Sprites/… to Sprites/… in your working folder (or from Dumps/Spritesheets/… to Spritesheets/…; paths reference). The path from Sprites/ (or Spritesheets/) on must stay the same. Then the file loads. Don't ship Dumps/ inside a pack — the Pack Doctor flags it as debris.

One frame in all three places: the dump, your working folder, and your pack. Each path starts in the game folder. The part from Sprites/ on is the same:

               Stitchwork/Dumps/Sprites/Knight/atlas2/idle0000.png
                     Stitchwork/Sprites/Knight/atlas2/idle0000.png
Packs/my-hornet-skin/Stitchwork/Sprites/Knight/atlas2/idle0000.png
